Kalam

Kalam is a quiet place to write a little every day: a calm serif editor, a calendar that fills in as you write, a gentle daily page goal, several journals, guided prompts, version history, and Markdown/PDF export.

Your writing stays on this Mac. No account, no analytics, no tracking.

This installer puts Kalam in your Applications folder and opens it when it's done.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Runs on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
